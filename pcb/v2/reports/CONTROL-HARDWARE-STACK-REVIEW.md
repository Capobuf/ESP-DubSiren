# Control hardware and module stack review

Independent read-only review of the proposed prototype assembly. Candidate motherboard bottom/top: **Z30.75 / Z32.35 mm**. Front panel: **Z40.5–42 mm**, with six diameter15 mm, depth0.5 mm front-pot spotfaces to **Z41.5**, leaving1.0 mm local panel thickness. Rear master: **RV7 (23, 9.5), B.Cu, rotation 0**, with the local rear wall reduced to Y0–1.5 mm. No design object was changed.

## Hardware evidence

The [Bourns PTV/PTT sheet](https://bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/ptvptt.pdf) says a nut and flat washer are supplied but does not dimension them. Bourns' [Sensors and Controls guide, printed page 66](https://bourns.com/docs/technical-documents/technical-library/sensors-controls/publications/SC_Solutions_Guide.pdf?sfvrsn=4) identifies **H-W09-MM** as the hardware set for PTV111. Do not infer its dimensions from another Bourns series.

A concrete alternative is **Alps Alpine RKZ00023UA**, M9 x 0.75: nut **11 mm across flats, 2.0 mm thick**, washer **9.1 mm ID, 14 mm OD, 0.5 mm thick**. Those dimensions are explicitly drawn on the [RS drawing linked to that exact MPN](https://docs.rs-online.com/a2bd/A700000012851535.pdf). It is a commercially listed kit; replacing supplied hardware is only necessary if the received parts do not meet the selected stack envelope. The drawing does not specify dimensional tolerances, so use incoming measurement rather than inventing guaranteed limits. The drawing was downloaded and visually inspected as `C:/Users/dot/AppData/Local/Temp/dubsiren-v2-tools/Alps-RKZ00023UA.pdf`.

The exact [PEC11R sheet](https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/pec11r.pdf), page 3, does dimension its supplied hardware: **M7 x 0.75 nut, 10.0 +/-0.5 mm across flats, thickness 2.0 +/-0.2 mm; flat washer ID7.1 +/-0.3, OD12.0 +/-0.3, nominal thickness 0.5 mm**. Its general tolerance note is +/-0.3 mm below 10 mm and +/-0.5 mm at/above 10 mm unless separately stated. Consequently do not silently assign a tight +/-0.05 tolerance to that washer.

The exact Alps hardware drawing is also saved in the repository as [`Alps-RKZ00023UA-hardware.pdf`](../sources/Alps-RKZ00023UA-hardware.pdf), downloaded from the cited RS drawing URL and visually inspected. The identifier on its top row is RKZ00023UA / RS220-1686; do not substitute the M8 or M7 rows below it.

## Nominal feasible control stack

| Item | Body/bushing base | Thread end | Shaft end | Thread beyond outside panel |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Front PTV111-3, at spotface | Z39.85 | Z44.85 | Z59.85 | 3.35 mm |
| PEC11R-4120F | Z38.85 | Z45.85 | Z58.85, released | 3.85 mm |
| Rear PTV111-1, shaft toward -Y | Y2.0 | Y-3.0 | Y-18.0 | 3.00 mm |

The rear shaft axis is **Z20.75 mm**, because the motherboard underside is now Z30.75 and the axis is10 mm below that face. Do not retain Z19 from the previous lower motherboard study.

With a measured 2.0 mm nut and 0.5 mm washer, nominal thread beyond the nut is front 0.85, encoder 1.35 and rear 0.50 mm. These are positive nominal fits, not a worst-case tolerance proof. Use the whole nut thickness engaged; a positive surplus is preferable to accepting an arbitrary partial thread count. The selected diameter15 mm spotface accommodates the nominal diameter14 mm washer with0.5 mm radial clearance. Keep the nut/washer entirely seated on the flat recess floor; chamfer, tool radius and washer tolerance must not make the washer bridge the rim. Provide an assembly tool that fits the recess. The resulting1.0 mm local wall needs prototype stiffness/crack/creep validation with the chosen enclosure material and collar support; the spotface must not become an unsupported flexure under nut torque.

**Provide reaction spacers for clamping.** The body-to-panel gaps are front 0.65, encoder 1.65 and rear 0.50 mm. If the PCB is already fixed to rigid mounting posts, tightening a panel nut without filling those gaps can bend the PCB or load soldered terminals. Add measured insulating annular shims/collars at the bushing base, with appropriate bore and support area, to close each local gap without loading the shaft. The external washer then spreads the nut force into the panel. Use low assembly torque appropriate to the plastic PTV bushing; the PEC11R's metal-bushing torque limit is not a PTV torque specification.

For each received part, measure seating-plane-to-thread-end distance `H`, actual PCB face-to-panel-outside distance `D` (to the spotface floor for front pots), nut thickness `N`, and external washer thickness `W`. Accept only if `H - D >= N + W` with a positive assembly margin and no body/panel interference. Record these measurements before machining a fixed final support height. A prototype target of at least 0.10 mm surplus is an engineering acceptance choice, not a manufacturer limit. For the front nominal 0.85 mm surplus, the combined adverse stack deviation may therefore consume no more than 0.75 mm; rear's nominal0.50 mm permits 0.40 mm. Provide adjustable/shimmable PCB support or local panel relief if received tolerances consume that budget. The unspecified PTV axial tolerances prevent calling the candidate a guaranteed all-parts fit.

## Knobs and travel

**Davies 1100:** the saved [manufacturer instrument-knob catalog](https://app.mectronic.com/dsheet/1145_Davies-Molding-LLC.pdf) gives diameter25/32 inch =19.84375 mm and overall height15/32 inch =11.90625 mm. Its quarter-inch brass insert and set screw fit is intentionally a prototype check on the nominal6 mm Bourns shaft. Catalog dimension D =13/32 inch =10.31875 mm describes the section's internal height, but is not a separately toleranced shaft-engagement specification. Do not seat the knob flush to the panel on the 20 mm shaft by assumption. At the candidate shaft tip Z59.85, a measured8–10 mm insertion would put the knob's bore entrance near Z49.85–51.85, comfortably above the nut; verify actual bore entrance, set-screw height, bottoming and grip on the D-flat. This is an assembly adjustment, not a reason to change the PCB footprint.

**CLIFF CL170805B:** the saved [K85M manufacturer drawing](https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/6166/Knob%20K85M.pdf) gives diameter14 mm, height17.8 mm, a6 mm D bore with5 mm across-flat callout, a2.5 mm base recess and12.5 mm from base to the internal shoulder. The PEC11R shaft is6.0 +/-0.1 mm with4.5 mm flat height and10 mm flat length for L20. The difference between those flat callouts means tight retention is not proven merely by calling both '6 mm D'; preserve the user's physical-fit check. A nominal knob-base Z46.35 (shaft tip58.85 minus12.5) leaves4.35 mm to the outer panel. Even the stated0.8 mm maximum switch travel leaves3.55 mm nominal panel clearance. Confirm actual insertion stop and retention; do not press the knob down against the nut or eliminate switch travel.

**Same Sky SAK-023:** the saved [manufacturer drawing](https://www.sameskydevices.com/product/resource/sak-023.pdf) gives shell height14.0 +/-0.08 mm, bottom boss projection1.1 +/-0.08, maximum diameter14.5 +/-0.1, and the internal shoulder7.1 +/-0.08 above the skirt base. The total external envelope therefore includes the boss: nominal15.1 mm, not14 mm. The recommended mating shaft range is diameter5.96–5.98 mm / flat4.46–4.48 mm; Bourns' broader plastic-shaft tolerance is not automatically an interference-fit guarantee. Use the selected master knob and verify insertion/withdrawal, concentricity and torque transfer on the actual pot. With an8.2 mm nominal entrance-to-shoulder distance (7.1 +1.1), the shaft end at Y-18 puts the projecting entrance near Y-9.8 when seated to that shoulder; this is well clear of the nut at the rear panel. Confirm the shoulder interpretation and actual stop in the prototype before locking knob CAD placement.

## Display and DAC envelopes

[Adafruit5393](https://www.adafruit.com/product/5393) lists total product height **5.4 mm**; [Adafruit6250](https://www.adafruit.com/product/6250) lists **6.2 mm**. These are whole-module product dimensions, not certified maximum heights above their PCB mounting faces. They must not be added to a second module-PCB thickness, nor treated as the display-glass height. Headers, solder tails, selected spacing and any cover remain separate.

For the front, the available motherboard-top to panel-inside distance is8.15 mm. A 5.4 mm module envelope leaves2.75 mm total for bottom clearance, support geometry and upper clearance. One feasible **envelope allocation** is lowest display part at Z34.60, highest at Z40.00:2.25 mm above motherboard and 0.50 mm below the inner panel. The exact daughterboard PCB plane, header spacing and screen/overlay contact height must then be established from the real module or exact component models; this envelope alone is not a spacer purchase dimension. Adafruit explicitly calls for a cover or equivalent gentle retention to keep the TFT/backlight together. Make cover preload adjustable/compliant and avoid hard pressure on active glass.

On the bottom, reserving an additional 2.54 mm clearance plus the 6.2 mm DAC envelope below Z30.75 would put the lowest point at Z22.01, leaving19.01 mm to a Z3 inner floor. This is ample nominal depth, subject to XY collision checks and a selected header/support stack. Maintain the breakout jack's overhang, unpopulated auxiliary header assumption, connector mating access and solder clearance. Actual module heights, cover retention and knobs remain prototype checks; the board outline and accepted electrical lead maps are unaffected.
